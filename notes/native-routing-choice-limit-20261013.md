# Native routing choice limit repair

F01 expanded 53 eligible models into 424 root role/model choices (265 department choices), exceeding Jev's 255-choice limit. The repair preserves the original contract for at most 255 candidates. Overflow first selects an eligible role and lane, then selects a model for that role. More than 255 models are divided into balanced groups with at most 255 choices; each group contains the complete facts of every member. Selection recurses inside the chosen group until an exact model choice fits. No eligible models are omitted from a split and no fallback model is dispatched.

All stage distributions are retained under `hierarchical-conditional-v1` provenance. The final probability vector is explicitly conditional on the selected role/group, not a fabricated joint distribution. Effective confidence is the minimum of the stage confidences. Malformed stage choices stop before subsequent paid stages. Successful stage usage is summed. Caller selection, pool revalidation, breaker handling and role authorization remain owned by the native router. Partial failed-call billing and the existing single-call rounding boundary are separate F10/F09 work.

Failing-first regressions rejected root/department 53-model requests and a 256-model request against the real transport shape. Focused regressions now cover worker/department routes, 256 models, recursive grouping with 65,026 models, low role confidence and malformed role/group choices. Forty focused tests passed with 159 assertions. Typecheck and full build passed. The full suite passed 12,614 tests, skipped 20 and failed zero in 165.28 seconds. One extra malformed-group regression was added afterward and passed focused verification; production source was unchanged.

Real OpenCode 2.0.27 loaded the built plugin and completed root worker, root planner department and department worker routes with all 53 and all 256 fixture models eligible. Low confidence remained `awaiting_selection`. Actual Jev request state was checked for the complete model count, and every transport question stayed at or below 255 choices. Fixtures used local model/Jev endpoints and isolated test-home/XDG directories. Known fixture facts were preseeded to isolate F01 from F02 research scheduling.

An initial fixture run advertised only one model from `/models`, despite injecting more models. That run was rejected as acceptance evidence. The corrected fixture advertises the full catalog and asserts actual cardinality. Both accepted runs removed their disposable directory and owned process group, and preserved real `session`, `session_v2` and `session_message` counts.
